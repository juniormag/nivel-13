## Examples of well-written principles (delete afterwards)

- **The grid rules.** Between "it looks nice here" and alignment, we choose alignment. Test: grid overlay on, every edge lands on a column. Counterexample: translucent boxes behind the headline in v3.
- **Light, not paint.** Between a flat black background and a brand color field, we choose the field. Test: scroll the page; is there any section of flat black or dead grey? Counterexample: the testimonials section in v2.
- **Real before pretty.** Between the perfect stock photo and a real photo with good light, we choose the real one. Test: would any image pass for a generic stock library shot? Counterexample: the v1 hero.
- **The detail is the brand.** Between consistent detail and novelty per section, we choose consistency. Test: remove the logo from three screens; is it still recognizable? Counterexample: every v4 section with a different radius.
